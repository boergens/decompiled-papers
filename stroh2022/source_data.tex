\decompnote{The seven ``Source data'' files of Figures 1 and 2 (eLife, \texttt{elife-72147-fig1-data1-v2.pdf} … \texttt{elife-72147-fig2-data2-v2.pdf}). Each is an image-only PDF (no text layer, no numbers): one 2441 × 1370 px MATLAB figure per page and per sample. The only text is inside the images; it is transcribed in the descriptions below (read by eye and checked with OCR, \texttt{figures/sd\_titles.py}). The legend of each file, as printed in the article (main.tex), is quoted in the note at its start. All plotted data were digitized from the page images (tier 3, ``from the authors' source-data plots''): \texttt{figures/sd/<file>\_traces.csv} (all 12 line plots of every page), \texttt{figures/sd/<file>\_maps/} (colour maps, one grid CSV per map), made by \texttt{figures/sd\_extract.py} and re-plotted by \texttt{figures/sd\_plot.py}.}
\decompnote{Every page has the same layout. Top row: six plots titled ``x=200 μm'', ``x=400 μm'', ``x=600 μm'', ``x=800 μm'', ``x=1000 μm'', ``x=1200 μm'', each ``Intensity (a.u.)'' against ``Time t (min)'' (0--1200, ticks 0, 500, 1000). Middle row: six plots titled ``t=30 min'', ``t=60 min'', ``t=120 min'', ``t=360 min'', ``t=600 min'', ``t=1200 min'', each ``Intensity (a.u.)'' against ``Depth x (μm)'' (0--1200). Measured data are thin blue lines; in Figure 1—source data 1 a black model curve is drawn in every line plot; small black circles mark a point on some depth profiles (their meaning is not stated; for the similar Figure 2—figure supplement 2 the legend says they indicate axon tracts). Bottom row: a colour map titled ``data'' (``Depth x (μm)'' 0--1200 against ``Time t (min)'' 0--1200) with colour bar ``Intensity (a.u.)''; in Figure 1—source data 1 also a map titled ``model: R\textsuperscript{2}\textsubscript{adj}=…, SE\textsubscript{res}=…''; at the right two false-colour X-ray projection images of the sample titled ``t=0min'' and ``t=…min'' (no axes, no colour bar).}
