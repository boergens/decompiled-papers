# Yeatts 2026

MariPen Yeatts et al. **Scaling up polarization-sensitive optical coherence tomography to image the whole macaque brain.** bioRxiv preprint v1, 15 June 2026. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.11.731509). Not peer reviewed in the version examined.

**Screened, not fully decompiled.** Two entire adult macaque brains were imaged using a modified VF800, with 200 µm sectioning. The most useful evidence is the reported troubleshooting: 2% agarose allowed rotation and late-run gouging; 4% cracked; 3.25% gave the most consistent sections. Mold mounting and filling deep crevices improved support. The reported preferred settings were 0.5 mm/s and 68 Hz. [PMC article, preparation results and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308142/).

This supports feasibility with optimization, not an artifact-free or lossless run. Human-hemisphere accommodation refers to the custom tube's capacity; the demonstrated brains were macaques. The paper's conflict statement reports Precisionary internship/travel support for “MP.” [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308142/).

Figure targets: Fig. 2 embedding, Figs. 4–5 whole-section imaging, Fig. 6 interslice alignment; supplemental equipment details. Captions/text were read, but image-level inspection and numerical figure recovery remain incomplete.
