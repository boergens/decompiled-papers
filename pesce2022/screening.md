# Pesce 2022

Luca Pesce et al. **3D molecular phenotyping of cleared human brain tissues with light-sheet fluorescence microscopy.** *Communications Biology* 5, 447 (2022). [Publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03390-0).

**Screened, not fully decompiled.** Human regional tissue blocks of approximately 3 cm³ were sectioned using a custom VF-900-0Z. Methods report 450 ± 50 µm sections. These supported SHORT clearing, light-sheet imaging, and repeated immunostaining, including seven molecular targets over three rounds. [Results and methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-022-03390-0).

This demonstrates useful downstream processing of human tissue. It is not a whole-brain or whole-hemisphere demonstration, and it does not isolate the slicer's performance from the clearing/labeling workflow. No cutting yield, kerf measurement, or comparison against another slicer was established from the examined main paper.

Figure targets: Fig. 2 preparation sequence, Fig. 3 repeated labeling, Fig. 4 large human sections. Fig. 3d measures staining signal, not cutting damage. Main and supplementary plot recovery and visual audits remain incomplete.
